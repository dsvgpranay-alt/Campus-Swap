One Platform — All Your Campus Needs

Textbooks & Notes

Exchange previous semester books and study materials easily. Don't let your expensive engineering textbooks gather dust on the shelf when another student desperately needs them for their upcoming courses.

Electronics & Gadgets

Find affordable calculators, laptops, chargers, and accessories. Upgrade your tech gear without breaking the bank by purchasing gently used devices directly from your seniors or peers on campus.

Dorm Essentials

Buy or sell furniture, lamps, organizers, and room decorations. Hostels and PGs require a lot of setup—make it easier by exchanging items with students who are moving out or graduating.

Bicycles & Commute

Get around the campus easily by swapping or buying bicycles. A campus as large as SRM requires reliable transport. Find students selling their bikes as they graduate, ensuring a seamless transfer.
