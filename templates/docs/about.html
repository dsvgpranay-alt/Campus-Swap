Who We Are

Campus Swap was born out of a simple observation at SRM Institute of Science and Technology: every semester, students buy expensive textbooks, lab equipment, and dorm essentials, only to pack them away or discard them a few months later. Meanwhile, incoming freshmen struggle with the high costs of these exact same items.

We built this platform to bridge that gap. By creating a secure, student-only marketplace, Campus Swap makes it easy to buy, sell, donate, and exchange belongings right within your college campus. Whether you're setting up your first hostel room or clearing out before graduation, we ensure your pre-loved items find a second life.
