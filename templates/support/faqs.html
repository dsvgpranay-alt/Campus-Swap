Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about trading safely and effectively on Campus Swap.

Campus Swap is an exclusive, student-only marketplace designed to help university students buy, sell, exchange, and donate items within their campus community. Whether you need textbooks for the new semester, a study lamp for your dorm, or want to sell your old bicycle, Campus Swap provides a secure platform to connect with your peers.

Yes! Creating an account, browsing listings, and contacting sellers is completely free. We also offer a Basic tier that allows you to list your own items at no cost. For students who run small campus businesses or want to list many items, we offer premium verification tiers with extended features.

Trust and safety are our top priorities. During registration, we highly encourage (and for certain campuses, require) students to sign up using their official college email ID (e.g., @srmist.edu.in). We also allow users to input their specific campus, hostel block, and registration numbers to maintain a highly transparent community.

First, make sure you are logged into your account. Browse the marketplace and click on any item you are interested in. On the item detail page, you will see a "Seller Information" card containing their name, campus, email, and phone number (if provided). You can reach out to them directly to arrange a meetup on campus.

Absolutely! The core of Campus Swap is exactly that—swapping. When creating a listing, you can set the price to 0 (which will display as "Free / Swap") and clearly state in your description what you are looking to exchange your item for. You can also browse the dedicated "Swap Only" and "Free Items" categories.

To manage your listings, log into your account and navigate to "My Listings" from the top Account menu. Here, you will see all active items you have posted. You can click the "Edit" button to change the price, description, or image, or click the "Delete" button if the item has been sold or swapped.

If you lose access to your account, please reach out to our support team through the Contact Us page. Because accounts are tied to university email addresses, our admins can verify your identity and help you securely reset your credentials.
